# 01 玩家全身设定图（GPT Image 2）

用途：定玩家形象（V0.1 唯一 A 级角色），之后所有玩家部件与关键帧都以它为参考。生成 3～4 张，挑一张最接近验收要点的；不满意把意见告诉 Claude 改提示词。生成后放进 `assets_src/inbox/art/player/`。

尺寸建议：竖图 1024×1536 或更大。

## 提示词（整段复制）

```
Hand-drawn ink illustration for a 2D side-scrolling game. Dark sepia ink outlines (#2b2420) with slight hand-drawn wobble and varying line weight, thicker outer contour. Flat colour fills with at most one light and one shadow tone, subtle ink bleed and paper texture, no gradients, no painterly rendering, no realistic lighting. Low warm dusk sunlight from the LEFT: a thin warm rim light (#f4b860) on the left edges, shadows tinted cool violet (#7d4a8c). Muted, low-saturation palette: ink #2b2420, paper #efe4cc, dusk paper #f0cfb6, pearl #fbf6ec.

Character: a lone young wanderer in a post-apocalyptic wasteland, lean and agile build, about 6.5 heads tall. Worn layered travelling clothes in pale paper colours (#efe4cc, #f0cfb6) with ink-dark trousers and boots, a short hooded cloak or jacket, simple straps and pouches. One long red scarf (#b8382c) wrapped at the neck with its tail flowing behind — the only strong colour on the character. Holds a short single-edged starter blade in the near hand, blade pointing forward and down. Calm, determined expression, hair slightly messy.

Full body, strict side view facing RIGHT, standing pose, arms slightly away from the body, whole figure visible with margin, transparent background (or plain flat #ffffff background if transparency is unavailable). Single character, no text, no frame. No teal or cyan anywhere.
```

## 验收要点

- 深墨轮廓带手绘抖动，平涂，无渐变高光与写实光影。
- 左侧暖色边光，暗部偏紫。
- 唯一强色是红围巾；衣物为纸色系；**没有青色**。
- 正侧面朝右、全身完整、四肢与身体之间留空（便于拆层）、背景干净。
- 头身比约 6.5，体型轻快，能想象跑、跳、闪避的动作。
- 短刀为单刃、较短，不是大剑。
