# 03 无色实体（GPT Image 2）

用途：验证敌人能否与玩家一眼分开、且有自己的特性。生成 2～3 张，挑一张；放进 `assets_src/inbox/art/colorless/`。

尺寸建议：竖图 1024×1024 或更大。

## 提示词（整段复制）

```
Hand-drawn ink illustration for a 2D side-scrolling game. Dark sepia ink (#2b2420) with slight hand-drawn wobble and varying line weight, subtle ink bleed and paper texture, no gradients, no painterly rendering, no realistic lighting.

Creature: a "colourless entity" — a living mass of ink with no colour of its own. A roughly upright, unstable blob shape about as tall as a short person, edges bleeding and dripping ink onto the ground, wisps of ink trailing off its outline, two short smeared arm-like protrusions. Its only light is one small pearl-white eye (#fbf6ec). Almost entirely ink-dark with slightly lighter ink washes inside; a faint warm rim light (#f4b860) on its LEFT edge.

Full body, side view facing LEFT (toward the player), transparent background (or plain flat #ffffff background if transparency is unavailable). Single creature, no text, no frame. No colours other than ink, pearl white and the faint warm rim.
```

## 验收要点

- 几乎全是墨色，只有一点珍珠白的眼；没有其他颜色。
- 边缘渗墨、下滴，形体不稳定，一看就不是人。
- 与 01 的玩家并排时，两者剪影与配色完全不同。
- 朝左（面向玩家）、全身完整、背景干净。
